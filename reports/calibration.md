# Calibration and the clinical operating point

Generated by `python -m aptos.evaluation.calibration`.

Operating point selected on **out-of-fold** predictions at sensitivity >= 0.90, then applied unchanged to the held-out test split and to IDRiD. Selecting on test and then reporting test performance would measure the selection, not the model.

Chosen cut: **1.336** on the raw ordinal score.
