# Calibration and the clinical operating point

Generated by `python -m aptos.evaluation.calibration`.

Operating point selected on **out-of-fold** predictions at sensitivity >= 0.90, then applied unchanged to the held-out test split and to the external sets. Selecting on test and then reporting test performance would measure the selection, not the model.

Chosen cut: **1.336** on the raw ordinal score.

Where the grade goes wrong decides whether the referral does. At the top of the scale the external sets show IDRiD 8 grade-4 predictions where 64 exist; Messidor-2 3 grade-4 predictions where 35 exist - compressing grades 3 and 4 against each other costs no referrals. At the referral boundary, the share of true grade-2 (Moderate) eyes graded below 2 is IDRiD 21% of 156; Messidor-2 83% of 347. Those are missed referrals.
