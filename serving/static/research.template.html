What the evidence supports

Metadata predicts labels; external performance varies markedly; a controlled fine-tuning study supports a role for grading labels. These are distinct findings, with distinct limits.

What remains unknown

Prospective performance, patient-cluster uncertainty, robustness across additional sites and consistent grading standards. Duplicate-pair disagreement does not establish a formal accuracy ceiling.
